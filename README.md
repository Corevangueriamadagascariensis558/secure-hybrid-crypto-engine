## 🧠 Understanding the Technology

This software uses several mathematical methods to hide your data.

*   **RSA and ECDSA:** these act as the digital signatures for your files. They verify that the file belongs to you.
*   **AES-GCM:** this is a fast method to scramble the contents of your files.
*   **ML-KEM and ML-DSA:** these are post-quantum methods. These standards represent the future of security. They protect your data from future computers that might be faster than current ones.

The program combines these methods into one engine. You do not need to understand the math to use the protection. The engine selects the best method for your specific file type.
